# Code of conduct

ArkaOS follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

In short: be respectful and patient, assume good intent, criticise ideas rather
than people, and make room for newcomers. Harassment, personal attacks and
publishing someone's private information are not accepted, here or anywhere you
represent the project.

To report a problem privately, contact the maintainer through GitHub
([@thulasiramk-2310](https://github.com/thulasiramk-2310)). Reports are handled
confidentially, and the maintainer may remove content or block participants who
break these rules.
