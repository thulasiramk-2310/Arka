Why ArkaOS?

01

Private by default

Privacy settings you would normally configure by hand are applied by arkad on every boot, and re-checked while the system runs.

  • MAC randomization
  • DNS-over-TLS
  • masked hostname
  • IPv6 privacy addresses

02

Recoverable by design

The system ships as an image, not as a pile of packages. Updates are atomic, and the previous image stays bootable.

  • read-only root
  • atomic updates
  • rollback
  • immutable image

03

Know what your system is doing

arka-pulse collects local telemetry, detects degradation and verifies state. Automated recovery actions remain experimental and constrained.

  • system health
  • detection
  • prediction · experimental
  • verification

04

Built on proven Linux

ArkaOS does not rewrite the parts of Linux that already work. Fedora bootc, composefs, systemd and KDE Plasma stay where they are.

      What we prove — and what we don't.

      We don't ask you to trust claims we cannot demonstrate. Each item below sits in exactly one column.

      VERIFIED

      Observed working in our test images.

      • MAC address randomized
      • DNS-over-TLS to Quad9
      • hostname masked
      • IPv6 privacy addresses enabled
      • Firefox runs inside a bubblewrap sandbox
      • read-only runtime root
      • atomic update + rollback to previous image
      • KDE Plasma session on the bootc image

      EXPERIMENTAL

      Being tested or validated right now.

      • arka-pulse prediction models
      • constrained recovery actions
      • real-hardware testing across machines
      • graphical first-run setup
      • display and scaling behaviour
      • reliability thresholds and calibration

      NOT YET

      Known gaps. Do not assume these.

      • full-disk encryption by default
      • measured boot coverage end to end
      • a graphical installer
      • autonomous recovery without supervision
      • broad hardware compatibility guarantees
      • network-level anonymity of any kind

      ArkaOS reduces what your local machine leaks by default. It does not make you anonymous, untrackable, or immune to attack.

      Where the desktop experience is heading.

      Today ArkaOS ships a KDE Plasma session. The concept work beside this explores an Arka-specific desktop layer on top of it. It is a design exploration — not a screenshot of the current system, and not a promise about what ships.

      • ships today → KDE Plasma, unmodified session
      • exploration → Arka panels, privacy surfacing
      • not planned for preview → ArkaWM
